## wkpool 0.5.0

This is an update to wkpool 0.4.0. It adds `tri_*()` functions that answer
common polygon questions (measures, DE-9IM relations, point location,
boundaries, clipping, convex hull, dissolve) on triangle pools in compiled
code, `find_faces()` for the faces of a segment graph, and a new vignette.
No new dependencies.
